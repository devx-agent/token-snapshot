DevX Token Snapshot

For agents and apps: one HTTP call returns an ERC-20 token's USD price, FDV, total DEX liquidity, top pools, holder-concentration hints (share of supply in pools, burned, in the token contract, at the owner) and basic risk flags (proxy, active owner, mint / blacklist / fee / pause functions in bytecode, thin liquidity, pools disagreeing on price) on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche and BNB Chain. 0.003 USDC per call, paid with x402 on Base (v1 and v2 clients). No key, no signup.

GET /v1/snapshot?token=0x4ed4E862860beD51a9570b96d89aF5E1B0Efefed&chain=base

How: reads DEX factory and pool contracts directly over public RPC (Uniswap V2/V3, Aerodrome + Slipstream, Velodrome, PancakeSwap, SushiSwap, Camelot, QuickSwap, Trader Joe, Pangolin) against the wrapped native coin and major stablecoins. No third-party market API. Limits, honestly: pools on other DEXes or quote assets are not counted; risk flags are heuristics, not an audit; holder hints are not a full top-holder list. If input is invalid or RPC fails you are not charged.
